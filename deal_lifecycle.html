00

Big picture

PortLess is a merchant exporter in the middle. It buys from one Indian supplier and sells to one foreign buyer; the two never deal with each other. A scan finds opportunities, an operator presses Start deal, and from then on the system moves the deal forward by itself, stopping at approval gates where a person must agree.

Money moves only through escrow: the buyer pays first, the supplier is paid in stages as each proof arrives (PO, inspection, clearance, acceptance).

01

Finding opportunities

A scan checks every corridor (one product going to one market, e.g. black pepper → Germany). Corridors come from the Catalogue, or the defaults when it is empty. Each corridor passes five phases; it becomes an opportunity only if it clears every gate. Buyers and suppliers found along the way are always saved to Buyers & Suppliers, opportunity or not.

Opportunity statusMeaning
ACTIVENet price gap after duty is 5% or more and rank is at least 20. Shown on the Radar.
MARGINALNet gap between 1% and 5%: thin margin, shown with caution.
CONVERTEDA deal was started from it. A later scan of the same corridor creates a fresh opportunity instead of overwriting it.

Arbitrage rank = √(demand index × gap score); the gap score reaches 100 at a 40% net gap. A rescan refreshes the corridor's open opportunity rather than adding a duplicate.

04

Negotiation

The negotiation agent drafts each email to the buyer from the cost envelope (supplier price, freight, inspection, duty, margin floor). Every draft passes the SEND_NEGOTIATION_EMAIL gate; the first outbound email always waits for a person. A detected "yes" never closes the deal by itself.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> STARTED : buyer interested + supplier selected
  STARTED --> ROUND : first offer drafted
  ROUND --> ROUND : buyer counters, new draft
  ROUND --> HITL_PENDING : acceptance detected or draft flagged
  HITL_PENDING --> AGREED : operator approves agreement
  HITL_PENDING --> ROUND : operator rejects, talks reopen
  ROUND --> FAILED : rounds exhausted or buyer walks
  ROUND --> ESCALATED : handed to an operator
  AGREED --> [*] : negotiation.agreed
  FAILED --> [*] : negotiation.failed

AGREED is reachable only through the CONFIRM_NEGOTIATION_AGREEMENT gate. A buyer's price is checked against the drafts so a typo cannot close a deal at the wrong price.

Escrow, tranches, payout

The buyer pays the full PI amount into a neutral escrow. PortLess never issues a PO against money that is not there. The money then leaves escrow in four tranches, strictly in order, each tied to a proof.

Supplier payout sub-status

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> REQUESTED : payout gate approved
  REQUESTED --> PROCESSING
  PROCESSING --> COMPLETED
  PROCESSING --> FAILED
  COMPLETED --> [*] : total paid = PO value means deal PAID

Each payout is capped at the money already released from escrow. A partial payout leaves the deal CLEARED and a balance payout gate follows.

No tranche moves while the deal is in DISPUTE, CANCELLED, REFUNDED or QC_FAILED. Money gates are capped at L1 (a person, or a timeout that defaults to REJECT) and need two different approvers above the four-eyes threshold.

08

Approvals (gates)

Every risky step is a gate. Its autonomy level decides whether a person is asked. You change levels on the Autonomy Settings page.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> PENDING : L0 or L1
  [*] --> AUTO_APPROVED : L2 or L3
  PENDING --> APPROVED : operator approves
  PENDING --> REJECTED : operator rejects
  PENDING --> TIMED_OUT : L1 timeout, default applied
  APPROVED --> [*] : action runs
  AUTO_APPROVED --> [*] : action runs
  REJECTED --> [*] : soft reject stays put, hard reject raises an exception

09

Exceptions & playbooks

Anything that goes wrong (a rejected gate, a stalled deal, a failed inspection, unpaid escrow) raises a deal.exception. The Exception Resolver first tries a fixed playbook, then asks the LLM for a plan.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> OPEN : deal.exception
  OPEN --> ANALYZING : playbook or LLM plan
  ANALYZING --> REMEDIATING : AUTO_FIX
  REMEDIATING --> RESOLVED
  ANALYZING --> RESOLVED : MONITOR
  ANALYZING --> ESCALATED : ESCALATE or CANCEL, operator gate
  ANALYZING --> UNRESOLVABLE : agent crashed
  REMEDIATING --> ESCALATED : no automatic fix
CategoryPlaybook
QC_FAILEDRe-inspection (supplier reworks, new inspection, deal back to SUPPLIER_DISPATCH_READY) while under the limit; otherwise cancel and refund.
PAYMENT_FAILEDShortfall reminder to the buyer; after 14 days unfunded, an operator-gated cancel with refund of part payments.
NEGOTIATION_FAILEDMove to the next buyer candidate; escalate when the list is used up.
DOCUMENT_ERROR · CONNECTOR_ERRORAutomatic retry / regenerate.
FREIGHT_DELAY · CUSTOMS_REJECTED · MARGIN_BREACH · APPROVAL_REJECTED · CALLBACK_FAILED · UNKNOWNLLM plan; when unsure it escalates to you.

The deal watcher also raises exceptions on its own: PI expired, escrow unfunded, or any status stuck longer than the stall limit. It sends reminders before that.
