## Three moves that would raise profit per deal the most

1. **B-17: price every inspection the buyer asks for, and charge re-inspections to the supplier.** This is a direct
   margin leak of several hundred dollars on any deal with DUPRO or PEO.
2. **B-15: reserve RFQ slots for new suppliers.** The best price comes from competition. A capped list that only
   old suppliers can fill stops the price from falling.
3. **B-18 + B-16: one-click phone relays, each recorded in the audit log.** Local suppliers confirm by phone. Deals
   stop waiting days for a step already agreed, and every money-moving click has a name on it.
