## Accepted findings (ranked)

### B-01 — A supplier's directory usual price or an ex-works price counts as a firm FOB quote on the match board  (P0, Commodity trader, money high, risk high)

- **Evidence:** shared/orchestration/matchmaking.py:270-272 — any offer with fob_price_usd_per_mt gets basis "quoted"; supply_first.py stores the directory's usual price (source directory_usual_price) and manual_suppliers converts an EXW price without adding the road to the port
- **What it costs:** CONFIRM_MATCH can be proposed on a price the supplier never confirmed in writing, and an ex-works price looks FOB-cheap by the inland cost (USD 15-30/MT on a 350 km haul) — margin shown higher than real
- **Fix:** Give SupplierOffer a price basis (quoted / usual / estimate, FOB / EXW); only a written quote is "quoted"; EXW gets the inland leg (as the standby list already does) before any margin; a usual price makes the pair an estimate until the RFQ reply
- **Why it makes or saves money:** no deal is confirmed on a margin that disappears when the supplier quotes for real

### B-02 — Hand-made and supply-first deals never get the deal-created checks  (P0, Commodity trader, money high, risk high)

- **Evidence:** shared/orchestration/continuations.py:712 — on_deal_created only acts on DEAL_CREATED with status MATCHING; a deal created without an opportunity starts DRAFT (services/deals/service.py:529) and later moves to MATCHING by PATCH /deals/{id}/status or supply_first.start, which never emits it again
- **What it costs:** no trade-compliance check (HS export policy, embargoed destination), no counterparty enrichment / screening and no RFQ gate for a deal an operator makes by hand or starts from a supplier — outreach starts anyway
- **Fix:** Run the deal-created leg (enrichment, compliance.check_trade, outreach, RFQ) on every move into MATCHING — or emit DEAL_CREATED with status MATCHING from update_status / supply_first
- **Why it makes or saves money:** a prohibited product or an embargoed buyer is stopped before PortLess spends on outreach — or ships it

### B-03 — The audit log can lose entries when two instances write at the same time  (P1, CFO, money medium, risk high)

- **Evidence:** shared/audit_log.py:29 asyncio.Lock is per process; :86 insert_one({"_id": seq}) — two instances computing the same seq → DuplicateKeyError → entry logged as NOT recorded and dropped
- **What it costs:** a money approval or a Kill Switch toggle can be missing from the tamper-evident trail exactly when several instances run
- **Fix:** On DuplicateKeyError re-read the last entry and retry (bounded); and raise a deal.exception / Telegram alert when an audit write finally fails
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the audit trail a bank, auditor or the escrow provider asks for stays complete

### B-04 — One operator alone can clear a possible sanctions match  (P1, Compliance & legal, money high, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** services/trade_min/screening_review.py:57 resolve() — a single staff decision turns sanctions "review" into "clear" (verified, contactable)
- **What it costs:** a true sanctions hit cleared by mistake (or by one compromised account) makes a listed company a buyer or supplier
- **Fix:** Clearing a review needs two different staff (like four-eyes money gates); failing needs one; both stay in the audit log
- **Why it makes or saves money:** one sanctions breach can freeze the escrow, the bank account and the company

### B-05 — A stalled watcher is only reported in the next morning's digest  (P1, Operations, money medium, risk high)

- **Evidence:** shared/heartbeat.py marks a watcher stale; only shared/orchestration/digest.py reports it (daily at DIGEST_HOUR_UTC); shared/leader_lock.py fails closed when Mongo is unreachable, so every instance skips its ticks
- **What it costs:** for up to a day no reminders, no QC / freight polling, no lapsed-window auto-release and no approval timeouts — and nobody is told
- **Fix:** Push a Telegram alert the moment a watcher goes stale (checked by every instance, sent once per stale period) and when the leader lock store cannot be reached
- **Why it makes or saves money:** a stuck shipment or a supplier waiting for its money is caught in minutes, not a day

### B-06 — Fees recorded from statements never reach the ledger, the journal or the realised margin  (P1, CFO, money medium, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** shared/orchestration/fee_check.py keeps recorded fees in collection deal_fees; services/escrow_guard/service.py realised margin and journal.py use fees_total = provider fee % × released and portless_costs only
- **What it costs:** bank charges and fees above the quote are reported in the digest but the deal's realised margin and the books still show the estimate
- **Fix:** Feed recorded actual fees into the ledger (fees_total = actual when recorded) and post them to the journal (provider_fees / bank charges); realised margin then follows
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the margin per corridor that pricing learns from is the real one

### B-07 — A supplier on the do-not-contact list still gets RFQs, reconfirmations and best-and-final e-mails  (P1, Compliance & legal, money medium, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** shared/orchestration/continuations.py _rfq_candidates / _hand_added_suppliers (no do_not_contact filter; only _traded_suppliers_first checks it); services/trade_min/relationships.py:297 blocked_contacts reads buyers only
- **What it costs:** a supplier that asked not to be written to is e-mailed again — complaints, spam reports against the trade-desk domain
- **Fix:** Apply the directory's do-not-contact and declined lists to every supplier e-mail path (RFQ dispatch, quote_reconfirm, best_and_final, send retries)
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the mailbox's reputation is what makes every introduction land

### B-08 — Five money and risk switches are built but left off, waiting for a decision  (P1, CEO / growth, money medium, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** OUTREACH_VERIFIED_ONLY, RFQ_VERIFIED_ONLY (outreach.py, continuations.py), ESCROW_PROVIDER_BY_COST (escrow_guard/service.py), PROSPECT_CLEANUP_ENABLED (prospect_cleanup.py), SUPPLY_FIRST / manual suppliers without a buyer-side check — all default off or permissive
- **What it costs:** the protections and savings exist in code but do nothing until someone decides; nobody owns that decision today
- **Fix:** Decide each switch with a recommended setting (verified-only outreach / RFQ on once Apify screening is live; cheapest escrow when both providers are live; clean-up at 60 days) and set it in the deployed environment
- **Why it makes or saves money:** each switch either saves fees or keeps unverified counterparties out of the money path

### B-09 — The public rate limit trusts X-Forwarded-For, so it can be bypassed  (P1, Engineer / SRE / security, money low, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** shared/auth/middleware.py:90 — client = first value of X-Forwarded-For, which any caller can set
- **What it costs:** a caller rotating the header gets unlimited attempts at the webhook, unsubscribe and proof-link doors
- **Fix:** Use the address the trusted proxy appended (the last hop, or a TRUSTED_PROXY_HOPS setting) and fall back to request.client; never the caller-supplied first value
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the anonymous money doors stay protected against brute force and floods

### B-10 — Each module opens its own Mongo client and connection pool  (P1, Engineer / SRE / security, money low, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** 29 modules call motor AsyncIOMotorClient(settings.mongo_uri) (e.g. shared/audit_log.py, heartbeat.py, leader_lock.py, fee_check.py, outcomes.py, pricebook.py)
- **What it costs:** up to 29 pools × maxPoolSize connections per instance — Atlas connection limits are hit early on small tiers and serverless cold starts slow down
- **Fix:** One shared client per process (shared/mongo.py get_client()), every module takes its collection from it
- **Why it makes or saves money:** a smaller Atlas tier carries the load and the backend does not fall over under a scan

### B-11 — New back-office features have no console screen  (P1, Operator UX, money medium, risk low)

- **Evidence:** no calls in ../portless-ui/src/api/endpoints.ts for GET /hitl-approval/audit(/verify), POST/GET /escrow-guard/deals/{id}/fees and /fees/over, GET /health/watchers, GET /deals/price-alerts, GET/POST /deals/prospects/cleanup, the journal ?format=tally|zoho
- **What it costs:** staff can only use them through the API docs; fees go unrecorded, stale watchers and price moves are only seen in the digest
- **Fix:** Audit log + verify on System Health, watchers status there too, a fees form and list on the deal's Payments tab and Money & Margin, price alerts on the radar, clean-up preview on Prospects, Tally / Zoho download buttons
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the controls that protect margin are used every day instead of forgotten

### B-12 — Supplier e-mails have no unsubscribe and no clear opt-out  (P2, Buyer / supplier view, money low, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** shared/orchestration/unsubscribe.py is used by outreach.draft only; services/sourcing/service.py rfq_email_body, quote_reconfirm.body and best_and_final.body carry none
- **What it costs:** suppliers (often small mills) have no easy way to stop PortLess's e-mails; under cold-e-mail rules every commercial e-mail should carry one
- **Fix:** Add the signed unsubscribe link (supplier role) to RFQ, reconfirmation and best-and-final e-mails, honoured as supplier do-not-contact
- **Why it makes or saves money:** fewer spam reports against the domain that carries every quote request

### B-13 — Directory-wide scans on every request will not scale  (P2, Engineer / SRE / security, money low, risk medium)

- **Evidence:** 16 places read the whole directory (limit=100_000 / 5000): manual_suppliers.find_duplicate per added row, dedupe.duplicates, supply_first.buyers_for, offer_checks._directory_record, supplier_commitment._manual_standbys, outreach._directory_view (with relationships.annotate)
- **What it costs:** a CSV import of 500 rows over a 10,000-company directory reads 5 million records; the desk and outreach slow down as the directory grows
- **Fix:** Indexed lookups (gstin, domain, phone, hs_codes) in the Mongo directory and an index map per import; cache the annotated directory view per minute
- **Why it makes or saves money:** the directory can grow to the size that finds more buyers without slowing the desk

### B-14 — Decisions are typed into browser prompt boxes  (P2, Operator UX, money low, risk low)

- **Evidence:** window.prompt in Prospects.tsx (park / drop / retry), BuyersSuppliers.tsx (bulk), CompanyViews.tsx (do-not-contact, start a prospect), ScreeningReviewCard.tsx (clear / fail) — 9 places
- **What it costs:** no validation, no context while typing a reason, unusable on a phone; reasons end up as one word
- **Fix:** One small reason dialog component (reason, optional date / market, the consequence spelled out) used everywhere
- **Why it makes or saves money:** faster, better-documented decisions on the go

## Rejected

- B-15 LLM cost is not in the cost per paid deal — already in todo (Cost dashboards: LLM, Apify, Tavily, Atlas, hosting)
- B-16 QIMA results only arrive by polling — already in todo (QIMA has no webhook — confirm acceptable delay)

## The three moves that raise profit per deal the most

1. **B-01 / B-02 first** — only confirmed FOB prices make a pair firm, and every deal (hand-made, supply-first) goes through the creation checks. Both protect the margin and the licence on the very first trade.
2. **Decide the five switches (B-07)** — verified-only outreach / RFQ keeps unverified companies out of the money path; cheapest escrow saves ~0.1 % of every deal once both providers are live.
3. **Put the back-office tools on screen (B-10)** — recorded fees, price alerts and the audit log only protect margin if staff see them daily.

## Learned (added to the members' checklists)

- trader: check how every *price source* is labelled (quoted / usual / estimate, FOB / EXW) before trusting a margin.
- compliance / trader: every path into MATCHING must run the creation checks — test the operator and supply-first paths, not only the radar path.
- engineer: anything keyed on a client header (IP, user agent) must come from the trusted proxy.
- operator: every new backend endpoint needs a console entry point in the same batch.
